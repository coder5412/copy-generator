# Task 1.2 — Advanced Prompt Engineering Challenge

## Prompt 1 — Social Media Post (Rewritten)

1. Highlights our new shoe launch.
2. Appeals to environmentally conscious, style-savvy young adults (ages 18-35).
3. Uses a friendly, playful tone with a clear call-to-action to visit our website.
Include a hashtag strategy with 3-5 relevant hashtags.

Techniques Used:

Role assignment: Positioned the AI as a social media marketing strategist to guide voice and tone.
Output constraints: Specified sentence count, audience, tone, and hashtag inclusion to focus results.
Contextual cues: Added target demographic and eco-friendly positioning to improve relevance.
Explicit instructions: Clear call-to-action and hashtag requirements reduce vagueness.

Before/After Comparison

Weak Prompt Output:
"Check out our new shoes! They are great and stylish. Buy now!"

Rewritten Prompt Output:
"Step into style and sustainability! 🌿 Our newest eco-friendly kicks just dropped, perfect for the young trendsetters who care about the planet. Limited stock—grab your pair today and walk the talk! 👟 #EcoChic #SustainableStyle #NewKicks #StepGreen"

## Prompt 2 — Creative Ad Copy (Rewritten)
You are a senior advertising copywriter known for creating highly imaginative and viral ad campaigns. Take the following existing ad copy and rewrite it to make it more creative, engaging, and memorable. Preserve the core product message but: 
- Use vivid imagery, metaphors, or humor where appropriate.
- Target a youthful, trend-conscious audience.
- Limit the result to 2-3 punchy sentences that could fit in a social media ad.
- Provide two alternative versions for A/B testing.

Techniques Used:

Role assignment: Positions AI as an expert copywriter for creativity and style.
Explicit constraints: Sentence limit and A/B testing alternatives improve usability.
Stylistic cues: Imagery, humor, and metaphors guide imaginative output.
Audience context: Tailoring for young, trend-conscious consumers increases relevance.

Before/After Comparison

Weak Prompt Output:
"Our product is great and works well. Buy it today."

Rewritten Prompt Output:
"Turn heads wherever you go—our product isn’t just smart, it’s a style revolution. Don’t just use it, flaunt it!"

## Prompt 3 — Campaign Brief Summary (Rewritten)
You are a professional marketing analyst. Summarize the following campaign brief in 4-5 concise bullet points that: 
- Highlight the campaign objectives, target audience, key messaging, and deliverables.
- Maintain the original tone and strategic intent.
- Use clear, precise language suitable for a quick executive overview.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon or filler sentences.

Techniques Used:

Role assignment: Makes AI think like an analyst, not just a summarizer.
Structured output: Bullet points enforce clarity and digestibility.
Content focus: Specifies which elements to capture (objectives, audience, messaging, deliverables).
Style and clarity constraints: Preserves tone and avoids fluff.

Before/After Comparison

Weak Prompt Output:
"This campaign is about selling our products to people. We have some ads and a website. Make it work."
